\subsection{Computational Overhead}

\texttt{ob} provides read-only queries \texttt{blame FILE LINE} (line-level attribution) and \texttt{show} (records by author/section), plus the \texttt{revoke} mutation (Section~2.2). At 220k records, \texttt{blame} takes 0.66\,ms, \texttt{show} 178\,ms, and \texttt{revoke} 29\,ms. On zhwiki data (1k--220k records), pipeline integration adds 1--2\% throughput overhead (HuggingFace) and 9--21\% (Datatrove). Storage overhead (total/data ratio) decreases with scale: Datatrove from 2.7$\times$ to 1.6$\times$, HuggingFace from 2.4$\times$ to 1.7$\times$. The overhead is higher at small scales because provenance metadata is amortized over fewer records; in streaming mode, it drops to $\sim$1.2$\times$. We retain plain-JSONL storage for human-readability and Git-trackability, which compliance audits require. Token-level queries add sub-150\,ms latency for 712M tokens.

\subsection{Machine Unlearning Validation}

We fine-tune Qwen3-1.7B~\cite{qwen3_2025} on ChatML QA data generated from 10k zhwiki pages and apply NPO~\cite{zhang2024npo} under three forget-set conditions: line-level provenance (record-level revocation), page-level prototype (all records from co-authored pages), and random baseline. Table~\ref{tab:mu-results} reports per-author results. Under NPO, line-level provenance sets achieve lower retain perplexity (PPL) than random---up to 12\% reduction (Ohtashinichiro: 6.5 vs.\ 7.4)---confirming that record-level provenance identifies higher-quality forget sets.

\noindent\textbf{Limitation: transformation gap.} Extraction on line-level provenance sets remains high (0.50--0.79), confirming that provenance identifies the right records but the model has already absorbed their content beyond record boundaries. This gap is especially challenging for Wikipedia, where a single contributor may edit completely unrelated topics. Frequently occurring knowledge shared across multiple contributors is harder to suppress~\cite{krishnan2025notall}, and the influence of individual records on model parameters is non-localized~\cite{hammoudeh2024influence}. Bridging provenance with knowledge-level unlearning remains an open challenge.
