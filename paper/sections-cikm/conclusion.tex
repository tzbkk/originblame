\section{Conclusion}

OriginBlame bridges the provenance gap between contributor identity and training data through deterministic record- and token-level attribution. Record-level provenance reduces file-level over-deletion to near-parity (1--21\% pipeline overhead), and provenance-based forget sets integrate with existing unlearning algorithms. A gap remains: provenance identifies source records, but models store transformed knowledge beyond individual records---bridging this is left to future work. Our interactive demonstration lets attendees explore provenance queries, execute right-to-erasure requests, and compare deletion granularities in real time.
